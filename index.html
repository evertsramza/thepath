you, me and everyone else

we are all going somewhere

we are all on our paths to becomes somebodies.

and there are these spaces

that provide guidance

in shaping our paths

by affording access to their

structural and intellectual passageways.

they are both material and immaterial

both helpful and constraining

and are simultaneously guides and guardrails

steering you, me and everyone else

forward on our paths.

YOU'RE IN. NOW GO.
WHERE TO NOW?
